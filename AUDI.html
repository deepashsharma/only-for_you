    Why VAHDAM® India?

    Founded by a 4th generation tea entrepreneur, VAHDAM ® India is an award-winning, sustainable & ethical brand bringing India's finest teas to consumers globally. By eliminating all unnecessary middlemen, we are not only able to make available garden fresh, high-quality teas to consumers across the globe, but are also able to retain all earnings in the region where they are grown and nurtured by millions of farmers with immense love, care, and passion. A process which ultimately helps every farmer get a better price for their produce.
